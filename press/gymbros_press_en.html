About Gym Bros

Gym Bros is a gamified fitness app that turns your workout routine into a social, fun, and motivating experience.


Designed to encourage consistency, friendly competition, and social engagement, Gym Bros combines fitness tracking with game-like mechanics. Users can maintain streaks, level up, join challenges, compete in rankings, create groups, and follow their friends’ progress in a dynamic and engaging way.


By combining productivity, well-being, and gamification, Gym Bros aims to make working out more enjoyable and sustainable in everyday life.

Key Features

  • Daily streak: track your consistency and keep your streak alive with workouts and rest days.
  • Friend rankings: compete with friends and group members through dynamic leaderboards.
  • Custom challenges: create and join challenges based on workout time, frequency, or specific goals.
  • Social groups: organize your fitness journey in private or public groups.
  • Levels & coins system: progress through levels and earn rewards.
  • Avatars & customization: personalize your profile with avatars and visual elements.
  • Workout tracking: log activities and turn them into visible progress inside the app.

Development

Gym Bros is an independent project created by Raul Lermen (iOS) and Carlos Vaccari (Android).


The app was born from the idea of combining fitness, gamification, and social interaction to help people stay consistent with their workouts in a fun way.
